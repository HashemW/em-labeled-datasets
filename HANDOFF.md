## Gotchas found
- EBI HTML **and** FTP directory listings drop about 0.1-0.3% of files. The fix is listing union plus gap probing; slices are addressed by number.
- On Windows, tensorstore cannot reach buckets.embl.de (schannel revocation check). The driver therefore uses its own requests-based zarr/N5 reader.
- 4TU (FAST-EM sample zip) returned 503 "storage maintenance" all day on 2026-10-08.
- EMPIAR's `pixel_width` field is sometimes in Å and sometimes in nm.
- Under heavy parallel load, a few EBI responses had the right length but different bytes (cause unknown: server or local network). Payloads without a checksum are now read twice and compared; this was tested with injected corruption.
